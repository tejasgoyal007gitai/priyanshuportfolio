Introduction

I am Priyanshu Agarwal, an MBA Marketing candidate at FLAME University, Pune, with a background in business development, recruitment, hospitality management and government-focused market research.

My professional journey has helped me understand businesses from different perspectives – from identifying client requirements and acquiring customers to researching markets, developing sales strategies and driving business growth.

I am particularly interested in B2B sales, strategic account management, market intelligence, product positioning and business development.
