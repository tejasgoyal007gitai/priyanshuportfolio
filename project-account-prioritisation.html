B2B Sales Strategy

Strategic Account Prioritisation Framework

A systematic framework for identifying, evaluating and prioritising high-potential B2B accounts, with an interactive illustrative matrix.

01Project title

Developing a Strategic Account Prioritisation Framework for Improving B2B Sales Effectiveness.

Conceptual framework This is conceptual project work, not a record of completed commercial outcomes.

02Business challenge

Sales teams have limited time. Without a consistent way to rank accounts, effort spreads evenly across accounts with very different potential.

03Objective

Develop a systematic framework for identifying, evaluating and prioritising high-potential B2B accounts.

04Evaluation dimensions

  • Account potential
  • Strategic fit
  • Revenue opportunity
  • Profitability
  • Relationship strength
  • Growth potential
  • Ease of service

05Interactive prioritisation matrix

Adjust how much each dimension matters and see how the ranking changes.

Illustrative only. The accounts and scores below are hypothetical examples to show how the framework works. They are not client data or project results.

Set the weights

Total weight: 100. Scores are normalised, so the total need not equal 100.

Resulting priority

#AccountWeighted scoreOut of 5Tier

Each account is scored 1–5 on every dimension; the weighted average sets the tier (Priority ≥ 3.8, Develop ≥ 3.0, otherwise Maintain).
