IMC Plan & Promotional Strategy: AXE

Redesigning a brand's promotional strategy to change its image and positioning: IMC objectives, budget, media plan and creatives across video, print, audio and digital.

01Overview

The final examination project for the MBA course on Integrated Marketing Communications: completely redesign the promotional strategy of a brand that needs to change its brand image and positioning to win consumer acceptance.

02The brief

Choose one category from men's innerwear, mouth fresheners, fairness creams, toothpaste, perfume / deodorant or cement, then narrow it down to a brand that urgently needs a new brand identity. The team chose AXE in the perfume / deodorant category.

03Deliverables

  • Brand and ad elements, where a redesign is needed
  • IMC objectives and budgeting
  • Media planning
  • Ad creatives: a video ad (TV, YouTube or social media), a print ad (newspaper, magazine or OOH), an audio ad (radio or Spotify) and two digital marketing formats other than video

04Assessment

A 10-minute presentation followed by a 10-minute viva, counting for 40% of the course grade (October 2026).
